## Animations

These animations illustrate the parameter dependence of the lower bounds and normalized remainder kernels considered in the Brannan–Meijer paper.

The first two show positive lower bounds $\mathcal H_5(\alpha,\beta,\zeta)$ and $\mathcal Q_5(\alpha,\beta,\zeta)$ as $\zeta$ varies over $[0,81]$. The positivity checks in the paper use the simpler $\zeta$-independent lower bounds $\mathcal H_5^*(\alpha,\beta)$ and $\mathcal Q_5^*(\alpha,\beta)$, which apply uniformly on this interval. The animations provide complementary illustrations of the shapes and parameter dependence of the functions being bounded.

- `Fig1-H5-zeta.gif`  
  Animation of $\mathcal H_5(\alpha,\beta,\zeta)$ for $\zeta\in[0,81]$.

- `Fig2-Q5-zeta.gif`  
  Animation of $\mathcal Q_5(\alpha,\beta,\zeta)$ for $\zeta\in[0,81]$.

- `Fig3-tildeP-phi.gif`  
  Animation of $\widetilde P(\alpha,\beta,\phi)$ for $\phi\in[0,\phi_0]$.

- `Fig4-L0-s-rho0.gif`  
  Animation of $\mathsf L_0(\alpha,\beta,\rho;s)$ for $\rho=0$ and $s\in[0,5]$.

- `Fig5-tildeL0-cphi.gif`  
  Animation of $\widetilde{\mathsf L}_0(\alpha,\beta,\phi;s)$ for $\phi=10^{-50}$, $s=c\phi$, and $c\in(0,1]$.

- `L0-s-rhoPhi0.gif`  
  Animation of $\mathsf L_0(\alpha,\beta,\rho;s)$ for $\rho=2\sin(\phi_0/2)$ and $s\in[0,5]$.

- `tildeL0-s-phi0.gif`  
  Animation of $\widetilde{\mathsf L}_0(\alpha,\beta,\phi;s)$ for $\phi=\phi_0$ and $s\in[0,5]$.

- `tildeL0-s-phiTiny.gif`  
  Animation of $\widetilde{\mathsf L}_0(\alpha,\beta,\phi;s)$ for $\phi=10^{-50}$ and $s\in[10^{-30},5]$.

Here $\phi_0=0.061$. All animations display surfaces over the unit parameter square $\alpha,\beta\in[0,1]$.
